research

I’m a first-year PhD student in Computing and Information Sciences at RIT, working with Prof. Hwan Shim in the Music and Audio Cognition Lab (MACL). My work explores how people attend to and perceive sound, with a focus on decoding auditory attention from EEG and separating speech in real-world mixtures. Previously, at the Tufts HCI Lab with Prof. Robert Jacob, I used fNIRS to study cognitive workload in AI-assisted decision-making.
